# Design research method

Frame one decision the research should improve. Name the audience, task, platform, and constraints before collecting examples.

For each source record the product or document, direct URL or Figma node, date inspected, platform, viewport/device when visual, state or step observed, and a screenshot path when permitted. Label access limitations.

Capture observations in a comparison table:

| Source and state | Direct observation | Likely rationale | Transferable pattern | Risk or mismatch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Look for the whole task rather than isolated attractive screens: entry, orientation, primary action, progress, validation, loading, empty, error, success, return, and cancellation or recovery. Note how small screens, keyboard use, text scaling, and localization affect the pattern.

Synthesize:

- conventions worth preserving because they lower user effort;
- opportunities where current patterns underserve this audience;
- constraints the design system must encode;
- two or more plausible directions when the evidence supports different tradeoffs;
- the recommended direction and the evidence that supports it;
- unknowns that need a prototype or user observation.

Citations support observations. Product fit remains an explicit inference.
