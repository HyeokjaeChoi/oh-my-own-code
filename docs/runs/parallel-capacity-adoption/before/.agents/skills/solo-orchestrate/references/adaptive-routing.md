# Adaptive specialist routing

The user may delegate model and reasoning-effort choices to the coordinator. Apply any exact user choice only to the dimension and scope the user named. A user who explicitly asks for inheritance/defaults retains that instruction. Otherwise, under continuing delegation, the coordinator chooses each unspecified dimension for the bounded outcome. If delegation is withdrawn, omit unspecified overrides and inspect effective client defaults before claiming inheritance.

Choose the least expensive supported model/effort pair likely to meet the outcome and risk. Examples for calibration, not role mandates: Luna low for precise mechanical work; Luna medium for clear multi-file edits; Luna high for difficult factual retrieval or checklists; Sol medium for substantive judgment; Sol high for complex plans or independent verification; Astra only for demonstrated complexity or unresolved reasoning after correcting the approach. Use the minimum effort likely to work; verification depth follows risk and acceptance criteria, not model tier. Optimize total useful work and retries, not token price alone.

Record assignment, requested pair, authority (delegated choice or exact user request), rationale, and any observable result. Distinguish chosen, requested, and observed settings. Omitted model/effort resolves through client defaults and then parent; a model without effort uses its documented default. An unsupported exact user choice needs a supported alternative from the user. Under delegation, an unavailable coordinator choice may be replaced with a supported alternative, with the reason recorded. A transport or capacity failure is not evidence to upgrade: diagnose requirements, approach, edge cases, capability and infrastructure first.

Keep fresh specialist contexts, bounded path ownership, minimal necessary specialists, and independent gates. Do not pin model or effort by role or in shared config. Role files and coordinator metadata do not prove runtime identity. Do not claim measured savings, cache guarantees, or quality based on routing metadata.
